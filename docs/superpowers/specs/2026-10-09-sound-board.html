Laddorious Sound Board

Every game sound and both music loops, made in code with Tone.js. Tap a tile to hear it. Tell me its code (like D2) and what to change: louder, shorter, lower, less cartoony. The demo round plays a sequence of sounds in order over the match music, so you hear them in context.

Tap anything to start the audio.

Music

M-MENU Menu loop 104 bpm, C major. Marimba arpeggios over a plucked bass, a soft kick and shaker, and a little bell tune.
M-MATCH Match loop 88 bpm, calmer. Sparse marimba over warm seventh chords and brushed percussion, no kick, so game sounds stay clear.
M-DUCK Duck test Plays the face-off sting while the music dips under it and comes back. Start a loop first.
