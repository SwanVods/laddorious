# Changelog

What changed in each release of Laddorious, newest first. The game's lobby shows this file under What's new.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-10-17

- Anonymous match stats: every match you play, online or against bots, is recorded to help balance the cards. There's no account; your device just gets a random ID. Bots are never counted.
- The settings say so, under the version.
- The stats change nothing in how you play.
- Rocket Warp is now an epic card.
- Topsy-Turvy lasts 2 rounds.
- Cleanse is back in the deck.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-17

- How to play: a new button in the lobby explains the rules.
- What's new: this changelog, in the lobby.
- Rare cards are now gold and epic cards cyan. An epic ? tile glows with a cyan-to-pink ?, so it stands out on the board.
- The camera keeps every piece in view when it pulls back to the whole board, even after you zoomed in.
- After the break between rounds, the camera no longer jumps back to the last card's player.
- Back to my piece now always finds your piece, even on the start spots or during the break.
- A card that adds or removes a rocket or whirlpool: the camera goes to that tile first, then it appears.
- A card that hits EVERYONE or each opponent shows the whole board.
- A Shield goes up as its card plays, not after.
- New Game+, Fair and Square and Epic Battle! are out while a rocket or whirlpool starts on their tile: they aren't dealt, they fizzle if played, and the card pick warns you.
- Low quality draws no shadows under the pieces, for smoother play on older phones.

## 0.1.9 — 2026-10-16

- Card text says EVERYONE and Each opponent, so it's clear who a card hits.
- Switcheroo: after the swap, you skip your next roll.
- The board's paint runs seamlessly over the platform.
- A balance pass over the cards.

## 0.1.8 — 2026-10-16

- The board sits on a colourful painted platform.
- Soft shadows under the coins, cards, crown, flag and other props.
- Encore rolls its own die and moves you that far.
- Sore Loser's caption says you lost but move forward.

## 0.1.7 — 2026-10-16

- Private rooms: play with friends by sharing a room code or link. Bots fill the empty seats.
- Rockets and whirlpools by name in every card and label.
- Every card that moves you to a rocket or whirlpool warns you when it would fizzle.

## 0.1.6 — 2026-10-16

- The match menu opens the settings.
- The round timer always shows.
- The card list on desktop is easier to read.
- The card pick fits on phones.
- The 4th place step on the podium fits its label.

## 0.1.5 — 2026-10-16

- Boards never have three rockets or whirlpools starting in a row.
- A short break between rounds, with its own countdown.
- A new app icon: a rocket out of a whirlpool.

## 0.1.4 — 2026-10-16

- Every card is playable, including Sore Loser, Booby Trap, Upside Down, Encore and Second Thoughts.
- New card kinds: move to a place on the board, and guard your tile.

## 0.1.3 — 2026-10-16

- Cards that trigger later, when something happens to you, and cards that check your roll.
- Whirlpool lanes may cross.
- The last moment of a round holds on screen before the camera pulls back.

## 0.1.2 — 2026-10-16

- New cards: skip a roll, roll backwards, a shield against whirlpools or attacks, and switching rockets and whirlpools off.
- Card bubbles show just the card's name.
- Camera fixes.

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-16

- The full card set, with a card's own die shown when it rolls one.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-15

- The first playable Laddorious: a race to tile 100 for four players, with rockets, whirlpools, face-offs and mystery cards.
- Play against bots offline, online by quick match, or with friends over Wi-Fi.
- A 3D board with toy-like pieces, sound and music, on desktop and phones.
